# Guides and templates

Copies of the Mays Teaching & Learning Innovation guides and templates the workshop links to, cleaned for a public
repository. The originals live on the team SharePoint site and were not changed. Guides are the team's
authoritative documents; templates are linked where a step uses them.

Each copy had author and editor names cleared from its file properties, review comments and assigned-task records
removed, and links to the internal SharePoint site, share links, and private video links removed (the link text is
kept). Public links are unchanged.
